Export to Bedrock
For uneven ground: the foundation fills solid ground under the city (stone-brick retaining face at the edge); clearing removes hills inside the city up to that height.
Java: the datapack goes in your world's datapacks folder (Edit World → Open World Folder). Then /reload, stand where you want the north-west corner, and run the build function. Java villagers arrive unemployed and take the jobs the city provides.
In game: build_centered places the city; once it has finished appearing, populate_centered from the same spot summons the villagers and golems. Each export gets its own city id. Only loaded chunks fill — stand near the middle; rerunning is safe.
